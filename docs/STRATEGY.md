# SokoCircle: Strategy & Competition Documentation

## Problem Statement
70% of Kenyan SMEs fail within 3 years, with 46.3% closing within the first year alone. The primary reasons for closure are lack of working capital (30-46%), reduced customer demand (24%), and critically - lack of market analysis, strategic planning, and location intelligence.

Kenya has 7.4 million MSMEs employing over 14.9 million people and contributing 33.8% of GDP, yet most operate without structured business intelligence. 84% of Kenyan SMEs lack access to formal credit, partly because they cannot demonstrate market viability or location suitability to lenders.

## Mission
SokoCircle connects people to structured, location-aware business intelligence that reduces failure rates. Not just another WhatsApp group - structured knowledge, verified mentorship, location intelligence, and market gap mapping.

## SDG 9 Alignment
Target 9.3: Increase access of small-scale industrial and other enterprises to financial services, including affordable credit, and their integration into value chains and markets.

Target 9.c: Significantly increase access to information and communications technology and strive to provide universal and affordable access to the Internet in least developed countries.

SokoCircle builds information infrastructure that enables inclusive industrialization by giving MSMEs the data they need to survive and scale.

## Pitch
Hook: "70% of Kenyan SMEs fail in 3 years. 46% close in year one. Most die from lack of information, not lack of effort."

Ask: Funding, partnerships, pilot locations.
