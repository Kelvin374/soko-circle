# SokoCircle Pitch

## Hook
"70% of Kenyan SMEs fail in 3 years. 46% close in year one. Most die from lack of information, not lack of effort."

## Problem
Information asymmetry, no location intelligence, no verified mentorship, and no structured community knowledge.

## Solution
Location-aware, verified, community-driven MSME intelligence platform that reduces business failure rates.

## SDG 9 Alignment
This solution advances SDG 9 by creating a digital information infrastructure that reduces MSME mortality through location-aware market intelligence, verified mentorship, and community-driven knowledge sharing - directly supporting Target 9.3 (enterprise integration into markets) and Target 9.c (ICT access for small enterprises).

## Market
7.4 million MSMEs in Kenya, KSh 1.2T SACCO ecosystem, KSh 76.8B digital lending.

## Differentiation
Not WhatsApp - structured data, verification, searchability, and location intelligence.

## Monetization
Freemium + expert marketplace + B2B data insights.

## Ask
Funding, partnerships, pilot locations.
